## About

I build and run the systems I sell.

I am Tony Ai Black (Anousone Rabounthunh), founder and CEO of Dorkbouakham Trading Import-Export Co., Ltd in Vientiane. The company ships three connected lines: smart vending (HM Vending), e-mobility (HM EVs — Laos), and software / AI (Laoapps, LaabX, Laab Cashin, HM AI Monitor).

Most of my work sits where hardware, payments, and operations meet:

• Smart vending machines with Android / Capacitor / Ionic / Angular kiosks, Java serial/SSP bill validators, LaoQR and MMoney, owner dashboards, and franchise rollout  
• Fleet and device AI — realtime monitoring, grouping, sales totals, sensor health, operator alerts  
• GPS tracking and e-wallet apps used in Laos  
• EV and e-motorcycle import, charging-site planning, and battery / BMS education for a hot climate  
• Local AI on our own GPU server for analysis, product work, and content

I still write the code, debug the serial line, stand up the Nginx box, and sit with a machine that will not vend. That is the point of the profile.

I also write, record, and release music as Tony Ai Black — luk thung / Isan / rock — using the same AI + studio discipline I use on products.

Open to: vending and EV partners, payment integrations, fleet AI pilots, and serious build work in Laos and the region.
